Scenario Questions

Imagine you are forced to choose:
• Option A: A 50% chance to lose $1,000, and a 50% chance to lose $0.
• Option B: A guaranteed loss of $X.

What is the maximum guaranteed loss you would accept to avoid the gamble of Option A?

Imagine you are given $1000. You have the opportunity to invest any portion of it in a risky startup. One of the scenarios could occur:
• The startup has a 50% chance of failing completely and you losing all investments you put in
• A 50% chance of wildly succeeding returning 3x your investment

Either way, you keep the uninvested cash and the investment. What amount are you investing?
