HUMAN BRAIN VS THE STOCK MARKET

Thank you for participating in this study.

Our goal is to understand how humans react to price movements relying purely on intuition, without the aid of algorithms or external information. Inspired by the recent surge in day trading and sports betting, this study explores the psychology behind our financial decisions.

First, you'll answer a few quick background questions and complete a brief tutorial. Then, you'll jump right into the main task: trading on historical market data to make as much virtual profit as possible!
